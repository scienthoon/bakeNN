# Code of conduct

BakeNN contributors and users must be respectful, technically honest and
welcoming. Harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, threats, deliberate
misrepresentation of benchmark results and publication of another person's
private information are not acceptable in project spaces.

Disagreement about architecture, numerical behavior or evidence is expected.
Critique the code and claims, provide reproducible evidence, and give others a
reasonable opportunity to correct mistakes. Maintainers may edit or remove
abusive content and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when
needed to protect the project community.

Report conduct problems privately to the maintainer through GitHub's private
vulnerability-reporting channel when confidentiality is needed, or contact
[`@scienthoon`](https://github.com/scienthoon). Reports will be reviewed in
good faith and handled with as much confidentiality as practical.
